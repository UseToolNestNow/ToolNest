Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Information we collect

ToolNest's basic tools are designed to work directly in your browser. Information you enter into a calculator or text tool is processed by the tool in your browser unless a future ToolNest feature specifically says otherwise.

Personal information

ToolNest does not require you to create an account to use the basic tools available on the website.

We do not ask you to provide personal information simply to use the calculators, converters, or text utilities.

Cookies and analytics

The current version of ToolNest does not require cookies for the basic tools to function.

If analytics, advertising, or other third-party services are added in the future, this policy should be updated to explain what information those services collect and how they are used.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as ToolNest grows and new features are introduced. The updated version will be posted on this page.
